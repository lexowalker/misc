# Death Over Your Left Shoulder: video model test

Two 5-second renders of shot 3 from the storyboard for *Death Over Your Left Shoulder*, a 1:54 short set on a Sonora highway at night in 1966. Carlos is driving the cream Chevy II Nova and mutters, "Old Indian bastard. What the hell does he know?"

Both clips start from the same approved storyboard frame and use the same prompt. They were made in Flora (project "Highway Film") to choose a video model for the full film.

| File | Model | Output | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| `shot03_kling-v4.mp4` | Kling v4, image-to-video | 2208×936, 24 fps, 5 s, with audio | $1.23 |
| `shot03_seedance-2.5.mp4` | Seedance 2.5, image-to-video | 1470×630 (720p), 24 fps, 5 s, with audio | $2.47 |

**Result:** Seedance 2.5 holds Carlos's identity (curly hair, face shape) and gives a subtler performance. Kling v4 is sharper and cheaper, but his face drifts and his hair flattens.

The storyboard, reference sheets and stills live on the Flora canvas.
