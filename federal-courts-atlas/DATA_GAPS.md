# DATA GAPS

Generated by `python -m pipeline.build_docs` from `data/gaps/*.json`, plus the hand-written notes in
`docs/DATA_GAPS_NOTES.md`. Wherever a value below is missing, the UI shows **No data**. Nothing is estimated.

## Cross-cutting gaps (read these first)

Hand-written overview, last checked 2026-11-27. Each item points to the lane report that has the detail. The
per-lane records below are generated from `data/gaps/*.json` and are the authoritative list.

- **CourtListener quota.** The free tier answered HTTP 429 after about 50 requests in an hour and about 124 in a
  day (Retry-After 69,262 s on 2026-11-27). Seven districts (wied, wiwd, wyd, prd, vid, gud, nmid) have no recent
  cases yet, and many others lack the opinion or criminal follow-up query. `make cases` reuses every cached
  response and stops after 45 new requests per run by default (`CASES_RUN_BUDGET`); re-run it after the daily
  quota resets (about 04:15 UTC on 2026-11-28). See `docs/lanes/cases.md`.
- **No street addresses or divisional offices.** The uscourts.gov court finder's result pages return HTTP 403
  (Akamai) to the pipeline. Courthouses are therefore placed at city level (statutory places of holding court
  plus the FJC's historic courthouse list), `address` is empty, and no `divisional_office` records exist. See
  `docs/lanes/courthouses.md`.
- **No duty station for judges.** The FJC Biographical Directory export has no duty-station field, so the bench
  lists each judge's court but not the city where the judge sits. See `docs/lanes/judges.md`.
- **Territories have no annual population estimates.** The Census Bureau's estimates program does not cover Guam,
  the Virgin Islands or the Northern Mariana Islands. Only the 2000, 2010 and 2020 census counts exist. The
  per-resident rates use annual estimates, so they show No data for those three districts in every year. See
  `docs/lanes/population.md`.
- **IDB FY 2026 is preliminary.** The FJC posted files through September 30, 2026 on November 24, 2026. The AO has
  not yet published its FY 2026 tables, so these values have no published cross-check. The site labels FY 2026
  "(preliminary)" wherever it shows it. See `docs/lanes/idb.md`.
- **FCMS method breaks.** From March 2012 the AO counts criminal defendants rather than cases and measures the
  criminal median from the proceeding date. Case weights were revised for use after December 2015, and
  supervised release hearings enter the totals from FY 2002. Affected series carry a break note; ranks compare
  districts within one year only. See `docs/lanes/fcms.md` section 6.
- **USSC grouping changes.** The Sentencing Commission printed the four-way grouping (within, above,
  government-sponsored below, other below) through FY 2017 and changed its classification in FY 2018; the atlas
  rebuilds the grouping from published components for FY 2014 to 2025 and marks it derived. Early-disposition
  (§5K3.1) departures are reported separately only from FY 2006. FY 2004 and FY 2005 are pooled from two part-year
  tables. Offense categories moved from statute of conviction to primary guideline in FY 2018. See
  `docs/lanes/ussc.md`.
- **Mass-tort distortion.** One multidistrict litigation (MDL docket 2885) put about 200,000 product-liability
  cases on N.D. Fla.'s docket in FY 2020, many coded pro se, which also lifts national shares in FY 2020 to 2024.
  In FY 2007 M.D. La. recorded thousands of personal-injury cases as jury verdicts, lifting that year's national
  civil trial rate. These are counted as filed and flagged in caveats, not removed. See `docs/lanes/idb.md`.

## Lane: boundaries

- **american_samoa_no_district**: American Samoa (5 county-equivalents, GEOIDs 60010-60050) belongs to no federal judicial district; county_district.csv carries it with district_id ''. It is not drawn. _Reason:_ American Samoa is not part of any judicial district: no section of 28 U.S.C. §§ 81-131 or of title 48 creates a federal district court for it (its trial court is the territorial High Court).
- **cb2025_20m_lacks_island_areas**: The 1:20m Census county file omits AS, GU, MP, VI; it is not used for geometry (the low-res topology is simplified from the 1:500k file instead). _Reason:_ Census product scope.
- **hawaii_outlying_islands_not_drawn**: 28 U.S.C. § 91 includes in the District of Hawaii: Midway Islands, Wake Island, Johnston Island, Sand Island, Kingman Reef, Palmyra Island, Baker Island, Howland Island, Jarvis Island, Canton Island, Enderbury Island. None is a Census county-equivalent, so none is drawn or listed in county_district.csv. _Reason:_ Not present in Census county files (cb_2025/tl_2025 contain no U.S. Minor Outlying Islands); Canton and Enderbury are no longer U.S. territory.
  - districts: hid
- **legacy_files_unavailable**: Historical county files that www2.census.gov answered with a 'Request Rejected' page (HTTP 200) on every attempt: https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/PREVGENZ/co/co00shp/co19_d00_shp.zip. Legacy GEOIDs that appear only in these files are missing from legacy_geoids.csv. _Reason:_ Census CDN/WAF rejection; not worked around.
- **ny_concurrent_waters_not_drawn**: 28 U.S.C. § 112(b)-(c): S.D.N.Y. and E.D.N.Y. share jurisdiction over certain waters. Shared jurisdiction is not drawable as territory; maps show the county lists only. _Reason:_ Concurrent, not exclusive, jurisdiction.
  - districts: nysd, nyed
- **split_counties_whole_in_county_tables**: Eight counties are divided by a statutory carve-out: Park and Gallatin MT and Fremont ID (Yellowstone NP -> wyd, 28 U.S.C. §§ 92, 106, 131); Hoke, Moore, Scotland and Richmond NC (Fort Bragg / Camp MacKall -> nced) and Durham NC (FCI Butner -> nced), § 113(a)-(b). The district polygons are cut, but each county's row in county_district.csv (and its feature in the hi TopoJSON `counties` object) carries one district_id: the district holding the rest of the county (mtd, idd or ncmd). Any county->district sum, such as a Census population denominator or the county_equivalents count, therefore credits the carved-out parts to mtd, idd and ncmd instead of wyd and nced. The largest share is Hoke County, NC: 362.3 of 1,014.3 km² (36%) is in nced (split_counties.csv). These rows carry split_districts and split_note; no sub-county population split is produced by this lane, so per-capita rates for these five districts that use a county-sum denominator are approximate. _Reason:_ Census county rows are indivisible here; allocating the carved parts' population would need block-level counts (population lane), and area shares are not population shares.
  - districts: mtd, idd, wyd, ncmd, nced
- **statutory_places_not_named_gud_vid**: Title 48 names no place of holding court for the District Court of Guam or the District Court of the Virgin Islands; statute_places.csv has no rows for gud and vid. _Reason:_ Not specified in 48 U.S.C. §§ 1424, 1611-1615.
  - variables: statutory_places_of_holding_court; districts: gud, vid; periods: 2026
- **tennessee_river_waters_not_delineated**: 28 U.S.C. § 123(c)(1): W.D. Tenn. (Eastern Division) includes the waters of the Tennessee River to the low-water mark on the eastern shore where the river divides the Western and Middle Districts. That strip is not drawn; the Census county lines are used as is. _Reason:_ No allowed public source gives the historical low-water line of the (now impounded) river.
  - districts: tnwd, tnmd

## Lane: courthouses

- **american_samoa_search**: American Samoa (5 county-equivalents, ZCTA 96799) is not in the search index: it belongs to no federal judicial district. _Reason:_ No district.
- **court_finder_addresses_unavailable**: No courthouse street addresses or divisional-office records: the uscourts.gov Federal Court Finder result pages (/federal-court-finder/find) answer HTTP 403 (Akamai 'Access Denied') to this pipeline. Only its autocomplete name list was reachable (no addresses, cities or districts). Courthouse points are therefore city-level (Census internal points) and no 'divisional_office' records are produced. _Reason:_ Source blocked for automated clients; not worked around.
  - variables: courthouses.address, kind=divisional_office; districts: all
- **fjc_courthouse_list_coverage**: Districts with no building in current district-court use in the FJC 'Historic Federal Courthouses' list: gud, nmid, vid. The FJC list covers buildings, mostly federally owned; leased court space and recent moves may be missing, so the courthouse layer is not a complete inventory of where each court sits. _Reason:_ Coverage of the FJC list.
  - districts: gud, nmid, vid
- **mdd_southern_division_described_site**: D. Md. Southern Division: the statute names no city ('a suitable site in Montgomery or Prince George's County not more than five miles from the boundary'), so this place of holding court gets no point. D. Md.'s FJC courthouse records are mapped. _Reason:_ Described site, not a named place.
  - districts: mdd
- **no_place_named_gud_vid**: No point at all for D. Guam (gud) and D.V.I. (vid): title 48 names no place of holding court for either, and the FJC courthouse list has no entries for Guam, the Virgin Islands or the Northern Mariana Islands. _Reason:_ No allowed source gives a location.
  - districts: gud, vid
- **zip_codes_not_zctas**: The ZIP search uses Census 2020 ZCTAs, which approximate USPS ZIP code delivery areas. PO-box-only and single-site ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not searchable; ZCTA boundaries differ from current ZIP routes. _Reason:_ Only ZCTAs are public.

## Lane: courtroom

- **courtroom_defense_counsel_not_in_data**: The Courtroom's defense table has no measures of defense representation (federal defender, Criminal Justice Act panel lawyer or retained lawyer). The page says so plainly and shows sentencing outcomes and the civil pro se share instead. _Reason:_ No lane acquired a district-level series on defense counsel type. Nothing is estimated in its place.
- **courtroom_reporter_not_in_data**: The court reporter's seat has no statistics (court reporting and transcripts are not in any atlas source). The panel lists the atlas's own sources instead. _Reason:_ No public district-level series on court reporting was in scope.
